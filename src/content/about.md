# Hi, I'm Teddy

I’m an incoming Ph.D. student in the [Department of Sociology at Columbia University](https://sociology.columbia.edu/). I'm interested in science and technology studies (STS), sociology of knowledge, sociology of medicine, and sociology of pain. I hope to focus my research on the relationship between scientific theories of pain and capital punishment in the USA.

In 2024, I earned my [MSc in Science, Technology and Society at University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/sts/). For my master’s dissertation, _Does PredPol Dream of Objectivity? A Critical Discourse Analysis of Objectivity in Predictive Policing Promotional Materials_, I used perspectives from science and technology studies to investigate a particular predictive policing technology company, PredPol. I also helped produce the policy report _Visions, values, voices: a survey of artificial intelligence researchers_. These, along with other projects, can be found on the [links](/links) section of this site.

Prior to UCL, I worked in the tech industry as a cloud solutions architect and earned a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics at the University of Chicago.
